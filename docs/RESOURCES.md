# External resources

Official and widely used starting points (not affiliated with this repo):

- [Learn Welsh / Dysgu Cymraeg](https://www.learnwelsh.cymru) — courses, levels, tutors
- [SaySomethinginWelsh](https://www.saysomethingin.com) — audio-first spoken course
- BBC / S4C learner clips and Cymru Fyw news
- Duolingo Welsh — useful extra reps; pair with a real grammar note source
- [Omniglot Welsh](https://www.omniglot.com/writing/welsh.htm) — alphabet overview
- Geiriadur Prifysgol Cymru and online learner dictionaries for lookups

## Books often recommended after Entry

- A structured course book from your Dysgu Cymraeg level
- Gareth King, *Basic Welsh* / *Modern Welsh*

## Language technology

See **[NLP.md](NLP.md)** and [`data/nlp_resources.json`](../data/nlp_resources.json) for CyTag, Cysill, CorCenCC, Trawsgrifiwr, Macsen, Iriaith models, and eval suites.

## How this repo relates

This project is a **structured dataset + tiny toolkit**, not a replacement for a tutor or a full course. Use it to power flashcards, SRS decks, bots, or your own notes.
